# STR Guidelines

## When to file
A suspicious transaction report is filed when, after review, the analyst and officer still cannot explain the activity with a legitimate purpose. Suspicion is enough. Proof of a crime is not required. Reports are filed promptly once the decision is made.

## What to include
Identify the customer and the account, describe the activity in date order, state which monitoring rules raised alerts, list counterparties involved, and say what enquiries were made and what the customer answered. State clearly where information is missing.

## Tone and wording
Use neutral factual language. Describe what happened and why it looked unusual. Avoid accusing anyone of a crime and avoid speculation about motive.

## Confidentiality
The customer must never be told that a report is being prepared or has been filed. Case notes and drafts are only visible to authorised compliance staff.

## Internal review
An analyst prepares the draft and a compliance officer reviews and approves it before filing. Any change made to a draft is recorded in the audit trail. Generated text is a starting point and must be checked against the case data.
