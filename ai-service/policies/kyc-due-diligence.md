# KYC Due Diligence

## Standard due diligence
Every new customer is identified with an officially valid identity document and a proof of address. The name, date of birth and tax number on the application must agree with the documents. A recent photograph or selfie is captured and compared with the identity document. Applications with missing or unreadable documents are not progressed.

## Enhanced due diligence
Customers rated high risk need additional checks before the account is opened. These include confirming the source of funds and source of wealth, obtaining senior approval, and setting a shorter review cycle. High risk indicators are residence in a high risk country, a cash intensive occupation, politically exposed status, a watchlist resemblance, or expected activity far above declared income.

## Politically exposed persons
A politically exposed person holds or recently held a prominent public function, or is a close family member or associate of such a person. These customers always receive enhanced due diligence and their accounts are monitored more closely. Being exposed is not a reason to refuse an account by itself.

## Sanctions and watchlist screening
Names are screened against sanctions, political exposure and internal watchlists at onboarding and when lists change. Matches are scored on similarity. Strong matches are held for manual review and are never auto approved. Spelling variants, transliteration differences and reordered names must be considered.

## Periodic review
Customer information is refreshed on a schedule based on risk. High risk customers are reviewed yearly, medium risk every two years and low risk every five years, or earlier when a trigger event occurs.
